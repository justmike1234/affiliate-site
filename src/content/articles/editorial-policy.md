## How this site is funded

This site is funded by affiliate commissions. When you click certain links and buy something, the vendor pays us a percentage at no additional cost to you. Pages carrying affiliate links say so at the top — that disclosure appears automatically on every page where links are paid, and it is there so you can weigh our recommendations with that fact in mind.

## What commissions never do

- They do not decide which products we recommend. Plenty of the products we write about have no affiliate program at all.
- They do not change rankings inside a guide.
- They do not buy placements. We do not accept payment for reviews or endorsements.

## Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix the page and note the change. If you spot an error, it is a service to everyone who reads the guide after you — send it in.

## Your privacy

Analytics on this site are privacy-friendly: no cookies, no cross-site tracking, no collecting of personal identifiers. Outbound affiliate links necessarily tell the vendor that a click came from this site.
