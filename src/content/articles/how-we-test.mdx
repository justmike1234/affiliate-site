Every recommendation on this site earns its place the same way: research first, opinion second, money last. This page explains the process so you can judge the results yourself.

## 1. We start with what people actually need

Before a single product is considered, we collect the questions people ask when they are about to spend money. What breaks first? What do reviews consistently complain about? What does "good enough" actually mean for the price? A pick that cannot answer those questions does not get written.

## 2. Only what we would buy ourselves

Each product on a shortlist has to survive three questions:

- Would we spend our own money on it at this price?
- Can we point to specific, verifiable strengths rather than marketing copy?
- If a friend asked us in person, would we say "yes, buy it" without hedging?

Anything that fails one of the three gets cut, no matter the commission rate. Commissions never influence rankings — the disclosure at the top of every affiliate page is the legal version of that promise.

## 3. We compare against the real competition

A pick is only useful relative to alternatives. For every guide we check what else is available at the same price, what it does better, and who should buy that instead. When a competitor wins, we say so in the guide. When the honest answer is "it depends", we tell you exactly which kind of buyer it depends on.

## 4. How affiliate links work here

When a product appears in a guide and an affiliate program exists for it, we link through the program's tracking system so the vendor knows you came from us. Those links carry an affiliate commission if you buy. The commission costs you nothing extra, and it never changes what we recommend.

## 5. We revisit and update

Prices move, products get discontinued, and better options appear. Guides carry an updated date when we revise them, and picks that no longer survive the questions above get removed instead of quietly left in place.

That is the whole method. If you ever find a pick that seems to break it, that is exactly the kind of thing we want to hear about.
